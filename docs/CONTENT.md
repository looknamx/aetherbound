## Traits

Each is active at 2 / 4 unique deployed recruits.

- **Emberkin:** All allies gain 10 / 20 attack.
- **Tideborn:** All allies gain 10 / 20 magic resistance.
- **Verdant:** All allies gain 100 / 200 health.
- **Astral:** All allies gain 15 / 30 starting mana.
- **Ironveil:** All allies gain 12 / 24 armor.
- **Warden:** All allies begin battle with 80 / 160 shield.
- **Striker:** All allies gain 15% / 30% attack speed.
- **Ranger:** All allies gain 10% / 20% critical chance.
- **Arcanist:** All allied spells gain 20% / 40% power.
- **Weaver:** All allies regenerate 8 / 16 health each second.
